# 2. Content Status Definitions

Every content item must use one of the following statuses.

## 2.1 Verified

```text
VERIFIED
```

Meaning:

- confirmed directly by the owner or authorized Gianetto representative;
- supported by a current approved business document;
- ready for production use.

## 2.2 Observed

```text
OBSERVED
```

Meaning:

- visible in a screenshot, social-media post, menu image, or other supplied material;
- potentially accurate;
- not yet independently confirmed for production.

## 2.3 Provisional

```text
PROVISIONAL
```

Meaning:

- created for design, development, or planning;
- may be used as temporary placeholder content;
- must not be treated as an official business claim.

## 2.4 Historical

```text
HISTORICAL
```

Meaning:

- appeared in an older source;
- may no longer be current;
- must not be published as active without confirmation.

## 2.5 Unverified

```text
UNVERIFIED
```

Meaning:

- source reliability is uncertain;
- information may be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect;
- not approved for publication.

## 2.6 Rejected

```text
REJECTED
```

Meaning:

- unrelated;
- duplicated;
- misleading;
- copyrighted without permission;
- unsuitable for the website;
- intentionally excluded from the project.

# 3. Source Priority

When sources conflict, use the following order:

```text
1. Written owner or authorized representative confirmation
2. Current approved Gianetto menu or internal business document
3. Current official Gianetto social-media publication
4. Current branch-provided information
5. Older official Gianetto materials
6. Third-party articles and directories
7. Customer or influencer content
8. AI-created placeholder content
```

A lower-priority source must not override a higher-priority source.

# 43. Content Risks

## Outdated Menu Risk

**Issue:** Menu screenshots may no longer reflect current items or prices.

**Control:** Require current owner-approved menu source.

## Branch Status Risk

**Issue:** Historical materials may show inactive locations.

**Control:** Publish only owner-confirmed active branches.

## Copyright Risk

**Issue:** Third-party photographs may appear reusable because they show Gianetto.

**Control:** Track ownership separately from subject matter.

## Event Schedule Risk

**Issue:** Live music may be irregular.

**Control:** Publish only individually confirmed events until recurrence is verified.

## Misleading AI Visual Risk

**Issue:** AI images may imply an inaccurate real dish or interior.

**Control:** Use as development placeholders and replace with authentic assets.

## Conflicting Contact Risk

**Issue:** Different posts may show different numbers or hours.

**Control:** Use owner-confirmed branch records as the production source of truth.

## Copied Story Risk

**Issue:** Third-party articles may contain attractive descriptions or history.

**Control:** Write original copy based on verified owner interviews.
